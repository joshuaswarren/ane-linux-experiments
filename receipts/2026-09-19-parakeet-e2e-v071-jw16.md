# Parakeet E2E wall on v0.7.1, jw16: `.ane` median 4773.8 ms — 16.3× the 292.2 ms M1-Ultra divisor (2026-09-19)

Lane: ParakeetPerf. Host: jw16mbp1-linux (T6001, G13C), single GPU window
under `/tmp/m1-gpu.lock` (inode 12, never stolen/unlinked),
`llm-inference.service` stopped before and restarted after — post-state
`active`, MainPID 394915, llama-server re-holding the lock.

## Verdict

**LAND (measurement).** On the published v0.7.1 wheel bytes, the full
Parakeet E2E pipeline on jw16 runs a warm median of **4773.8 ms `.ane`**
(runs 2–10 of 10; all-10 median 4779.4) against the macOS CoreML divisor of
**292.2 ms** (M1 Ultra/macstudio, `computeUnits: .ane`, same fixture and
transcript guard, `receipts/2026-09-16-parakeet-macos-timing-m1ultra.md`) —
**16.3×** (like-for-like without `audio_load`: 4698.0 ms, **16.1×**). The
v0.6.1-era tracked number for this host was 6418.5 ms (22×,
`receipts/2026-09-16-parakeet-e2e-both-hosts.md`); the improvement is the
conv-island placement (encoder_ane 5083.0 → **3391.5 ms**), consistent with
the AC-serve 3359.9 ms encoder re-baseline in
`receipts/2026-09-19-encwall-v071-attribution.md` (within noise).

The pure-GPU `.all` analog (`MLX_OMARCHY_PLACED=""` — whole pipeline on
Vulkan) runs **2165.9 ms** (runs 2–10 median; all-10 2161.1). It diverges at
the tensor level by design (matching golden prefix 97, status `diverged`,
all 10 runs) — recorded as a wall reference only, not a transcript claim.
Against the macOS `.all` divisor (305.8 ms) that is ≈7.1×, but the two
`.all` definitions are not equivalent (CoreML scheduler vs forced no-ANE),
so no parity claim is made on `.all`.

protocol (warm + 10 measured, median runs 2–10 quoted). One fresh
`fused_e2e.py` process per run; medians from `timing.total_pipeline_ms` of
each run's `e2e-report.json`.

Reading: the encoder wall (3391.5 ms) is 71 % of the `.ane` total and matches
the encwall-v071 AC-serve re-baseline (3359.9 ms, within noise); its
attribution (GPU matmul/const drains ~1950 ms + island round-trip latency
1291 ms) carries over unchanged. The pure-GPU encoder (849.7 ms) matches the
849.1 ms gpu-serve control of that receipt. The hybrid ANE path still adds
wall versus pure GPU on this orchestration; tdt_decode (~966–986 ms) is now
the second-largest stage and is the same GPU code in both arms.

## Window discipline

`TAKE` announced to live lanes before the window; no conflict. Sequence:
`sudo systemctl stop llm-inference.service` (active → inactive) → lock
confirmed FREE → one `flock -w 900 /tmp/m1-gpu.lock` hold for the whole
battery (22 runs, ~90 s) → service restarted, `is-active` `active`,
MainPID 394915, llama-server re-acquired the lock → `RELEASE` announced with
inode and service state. jwm1 and jw14m2 untouched.

## Not claimed

- No same-die macOS divisor for T6001 exists; the 292.2 ms divisor is
  cross-chip (M1 Ultra) and cross-OS (macOS 26.6.2 / CoreML 3520), so every
  ratio is indicative, per the standing caveat.
- macOS `transcribe()` excludes audio_load and model load; the like-for-like
  column removes `audio_load` only.
- `.all` arms diverge at the tensor level by design (golden prefix 97);
  their walls carry no correctness claim.
- The `diverged` prefix of 97 is recorded, not attributed; GPU-encoder
  transcript divergence is a known property of the pure-GPU control arm
  (encwall-v071 controls behaved the same way).
