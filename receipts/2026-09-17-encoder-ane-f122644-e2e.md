## What this battery does NOT validate

  - The o-proj plane-stride fix is NOT exercised. The 24 o-proj bundles
    (island-oproj-L00..L23) on /var/tmp/ANE-full-battery are probe/
    qualification bundles minted from ee2fdec/2d11b2a, predating
    f122644. They are not placed in the jwm1-encoder-islands/bundles
    directory, and the production encoder does not route o-proj
    through the ANE in this battery. To validate the fix in
    production requires:
      (1) rebuild the wheel from the new pin (.tmp-mlx-encoder-fix
          branch, ane-compiler.lock at f122644), upload, install;
      (2) mint 24 fresh o-proj bundles from mil-hwxc@f122644 with the
          fix in place;
      (3) place the fresh bundles in jwm1-encoder-islands/bundles/;
      (4) re-run the 6-run battery on the o-proj placement.
    That is a separate workstream.

  - The FFN chain plane-permutation analysis from probe3 already
    concluded (size-fit arithmetic, see receipts/2026-09-17-encoder-
    ane-probe3.md "Step-2 emitter port" section): the FFN chain section
    is the natural packer size, no 2x mismatch, MM1 hypothesis 1
    (apply o-proj 256-plane permutation extended) tested 0.94 vs 0.935
    baseline (rejected). The FFN bug is in a different class
    (silu/bias-add task word fold or per-tile header placement, not
    plane stride). The FFN chain on this battery is on Vulkan, where
    it computes correctly (the 104-token guard + transcript golden
    match proves the Vulkan encoder_hidden matches ANE reference).

  - The bias-immediate fix is NOT exercised by this battery because no
    linear/oproj islands are placed. It is exercised by the device
    gate (scripts/dev_gate_oproj.py on jwm1): worst rel_l2 = 0.000208
    across rngs {11,33,57}, budget 0.05. PASS.
