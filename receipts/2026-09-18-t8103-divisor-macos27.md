(the `.ane`-vs-`.all` delta flipped sign between the Ultra host's 3520 and nothing
here is guaranteed stable across 3520→3600). Whether a 26.6.2-matched erase-install
re-run on jwm1 is wanted for a generation-matched divisor is an **owner call** —
noted as a possible follow-up, not scheduled.
